\f0\fs24 \cf0 FIRSTBANK BUSINESS ACCOUNT OPENING POLICY \'97 Effective January 2025\
\
Required documents for opening a Business Checking Account:\
\
For Sole Proprietorships:\
   - Government-issued photo ID\
   - Social Security Number or EIN\
   - Business license (if applicable)\
   - Minimum opening deposit: $100\
\
For LLCs:\
   - Government-issued photo ID for all owners with 25%+ ownership\
   - Articles of Organization\
   - Operating Agreement\
   - EIN (Employer Identification Number)\
   - Minimum opening deposit: $500\
\
For Corporations:\
   - Government-issued photo ID for all officers\
   - Articles of Incorporation\
   - Corporate Resolution authorizing account opening\
   - EIN\
   - Minimum opening deposit: $1,000\
\
Processing time: 1-3 business days after all documents received.\
All accounts subject to identity verification under the Bank Secrecy Act.}
